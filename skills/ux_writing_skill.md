# Unslop & UX Writing

Edit text to remove AI patterns and add human voice.

## Process

1. Scan for AI patterns.
2. Rewrite. Preserve meaning, match intended tone.
3. Add soul.
4. Self-audit: "What makes this obviously AI generated?" Fix remaining tells.

## Rules

- **Cut puffery**: State what happened without buzzwords.
- **Plain words over jargon**: "use" instead of "leverage/utilize", "help" instead of "facilitate".
- **Say what it does, not how it feels**: Give concrete numbers, mechanisms, and instructions.
- **Active voice & natural rhythm**: Short sentences mixed with longer ones.
- **No robotic punctuation crutches**: Avoid em dash spam, excessive bolding, and fake enthusiasm.
